Behavior Cloning for Autonomous Driving

Imitation Learning • CNN Regression • Simulator Data • Closed-loop Validation

Abstract

This project implements a behavior cloning pipeline for autonomous driving using supervised learning. Driving demonstrations are used to train a model that predicts steering (and optionally throttle/brake) from camera images. The workflow includes dataset balancing to reduce straight-driving bias, augmentation to improve generalization, and closed-loop simulator evaluation to verify stability and recovery behavior.

Method

  • Collect synchronized (image, steering, throttle, brake) samples
  • Preprocess: crop/resize/normalize and remove irrelevant regions
  • Balance steering distribution + augment (brightness/shift/flip with sign fix)
  • Train CNN regression model; validate generalization to unseen segments
  • Closed-loop testing: evaluate lane keeping + smoothness + recovery

Engineering Notes

Behavior cloning is sensitive to compounding error: small prediction mistakes accumulate over time. That’s why balancing and augmentation matter—your dataset must include recovery examples and diverse conditions.
