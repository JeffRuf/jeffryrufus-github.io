Needle vibration on a breast biopsy robot

Which motions make the biopsy needle vibrate most? I ran a sensitivity study with regression and ANOVA on a multi-axis biopsy mechanism.

Report

Finding

  • Translational velocity terms dominated vibration in the tested range. Angular terms mattered much less.
