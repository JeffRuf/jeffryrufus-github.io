Vibration Sensitivity Analysis — Breast Biopsy Robot

Dynamics • ANOVA • Regression • Statistical Computing

Abstract

Performed a sensitivity study on vibration behavior for a multi-DOF biopsy mechanism using regression analysis and ANOVA. Evaluated the influence of translational velocities and angular parameters on vibration amplitude and found velocity terms dominate within tested operating conditions, providing guidance for future mechanical/control improvements.

Why velocity dominates (intuition)

Higher velocities increase excitation energy and can drive resonant modes more strongly. Angular parameters may have less effect if stiffness/damping and geometry make translational motion the primary excitation source in the operating region.
