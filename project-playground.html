Course project · ANSYS · SolidWorks

Structural analysis of playground equipment and a trailer hitch

I modeled four pieces of College Station playground equipment in SolidWorks, ran static structural analysis in ANSYS, and changed the designs until every piece had a factor of safety between 4 and 10. I also checked a trailer hitch model with a mesh convergence study.
