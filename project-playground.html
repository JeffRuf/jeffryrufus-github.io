Structural analysis of playground equipment and a trailer hitch

Course project · ANSYS · SolidWorks

Four pieces of College Station playground equipment modeled in SolidWorks, analyzed in ANSYS static structural, and revised until every piece had a factor of safety between 4 and 10. A trailer hitch model was checked with a mesh convergence study.

TypeCourse project
ToolsSolidWorks, ANSYS Mechanical
TargetFactor of safety 4 to 10
ChecksMesh convergence, load cases
