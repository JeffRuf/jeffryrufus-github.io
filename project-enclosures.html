EV enclosures

CREAT UNO Minda · EV Division · Pune, India

On-board charger housings and interior wireless chargers for EV platforms, designed for how each part gets cast or molded, assembled and serviced.

RoleMechanical Engineer, EV Division
PeriodJan 2023 to Mar 2024
ToolsCATIA V6, DFMEA, tolerance stack-ups
ProcessesAluminum die casting, PC/ABS injection molding
01

Die-cast aluminum charger enclosure

  • Reduced the mass of a liquid-cooled die-cast aluminum enclosure by 15% by thinning walls in CATIA V6.
  • Kept the sealing flange and mounting features solid by checking every change against the DFMEA and the stack-ups.

Result15% lower enclosure mass.

02

PC/ABS wireless charger housing

  • Cut the housing's mass by 9%, about 2 metric tons of resin per 100K units, by reworking wall thickness, ribs and draft from the tooling supplier's DFM feedback.

Result9% lower housing mass; ≈ 2 t resin saved per 100K units.

03

Design reviews

  • Presented enclosure revisions in design reviews with the CAE, tooling and quality teams, and updated the DFMEA with actions for the failure modes they raised.
04

Reverse Park Assist test rigs

  • Standardized Reverse Park Assist System testing across 3 prototype vehicles with vehicle-mounted rigs that held the ultrasonic and ToF sensors at design-intent spacing and angles.
