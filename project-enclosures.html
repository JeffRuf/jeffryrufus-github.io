CREAT UNO Minda · EV Division · Pune, India · Jan 2023 to Mar 2024

EV enclosures for Tata Motors

I designed on-board charger housings and interior wireless chargers for Tata's EV platforms, thinking about how each part gets made, assembled and serviced. These are company products, so I describe the work without photos.

Die-cast aluminum charger enclosure

  • Reduced the mass of a liquid-cooled die-cast aluminum enclosure by 15% by thinning walls in CATIA V6.
  • Kept the sealing flange and mounting features solid by checking every change against the DFMEA and the stack-ups.

PC/ABS wireless charger housing

  • Cut the housing's mass by 9%, about 2 metric tons of resin per 100K units, by reworking wall thickness, ribs and draft from the tooling supplier's DFM feedback.

Design reviews

  • Presented enclosure revisions in design reviews with the CAE, tooling and quality teams, and updated the DFMEA with actions for the failure modes they raised.

Reverse Park Assist test rigs

  • Standardized Reverse Park Assist System testing across 3 prototype vehicles by designing and fabricating vehicle-mounted rigs that held the ultrasonic and ToF sensors at design-intent spacing and angles.
