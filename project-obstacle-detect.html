Obstacle localization with ultrasonic and ToF sensors

A low-cost way to find where an obstacle is, not just how far. ToF sensors give stable short-range distances and ultrasonic sensors reach farther with a wider beam. With known sensor positions, the ranges can be turned into x and y coordinates of the obstacle.

  • Accuracy depends on beam width, surface reflectivity and calibration, so I tested at known grid positions.
