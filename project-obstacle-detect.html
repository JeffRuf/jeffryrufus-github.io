Obstacle localization with ultrasonic and ToF sensors

Course project · Sensors · Embedded

A low-cost way to find where an obstacle is, not just how far. ToF sensors give stable short-range distances and ultrasonic sensors reach farther with a wider beam. With known sensor positions, the ranges become x and y coordinates of the obstacle.
